Cyberbullying Detection Using Python

🌐 Empowering Safer Digital Spaces 🌐
In today's interconnected world, online platforms have become vital hubs for communication and expression. However, with this connectivity comes the pressing issue of cyberbullying, which negatively impacts millions of individuals. Our Cyberbullying Detection System is designed to identify and mitigate harmful content, promoting a safer and more respectful digital environment.
Our system uses cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to detect cyberbullying in real-time. It works seamlessly with code-mixed Hinglish (a blend of Hindi and English) and incorporates emoji-based analysis to accurately understand and classify online messages.
