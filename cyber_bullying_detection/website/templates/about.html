About Our Cyberbullying Detection System

Key Features:

    Hinglish Support: Tailored for Hindi-English code-mixed text, ensuring inclusivity for diverse user communities.
    Emoji Understanding: Advanced recognition of emojis to capture hidden emotions in digital conversations.
    Real-Time Detection: Quick and accurate identification of bullying content to promote prompt intervention.
    User-Friendly Interface: Designed for ease of use, enabling individuals, organizations, and developers to integrate the system effortlessly.

Our Mission

The heart of this project is to creating inclusive, respectful, and positive online spaces for all. By leveraging technology, we aim to reduce the prevalence of cyberbullying and foster healthier digital communities. We hope to hear from you..!!
